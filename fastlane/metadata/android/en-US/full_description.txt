Mapas is an offline, private map app. The map, the search and the routes are all computed on your phone: no live traffic, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. It works without Google Play Services.

This is a release candidate of an MVP, not a finished app. What works today:

• Offline vector map (MapLibre + PMTiles), light and dark theme.
• Region downloads from the Maps screen: resumable and verified with SHA-256. A no-network mode blocks every connection of the app.
• Offline search for places and addresses, powered by the CoMaps engine.
• Route preview by car, on foot or by bike (no turn-by-turn guidance yet).
• Saved places and lists, with GPX and KML import and export.
• Opens Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze and geo: links.

Not there yet: turn-by-turn voice navigation, lane guidance and speed limits, a motorcycle profile and twisty routes, track recording, Nextcloud sync, Google Takeout import, Android Auto.

Only Spain is available for now. Map data comes from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and is downloaded only when you ask. Free software, GPL-3.0-or-later.
