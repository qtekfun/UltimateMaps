# Appearance: map style and cursor (plan, 2026-10-10)

Request (owner, after the road tests of rc.18): the dark map is too dark; let the map style be customised; let the cursor be
customised too. **Status: planned, nothing built yet.** Decisions marked *open* wait for the owner.

## What exists

- The light and dark styles are generated by `scripts/gen-map-style.mjs` from `@protomaps/basemaps` (BSD-3) and packaged as templates in
  `app/src/main/assets/map/style-{light,dark}.json` (explicit colours: the dark one has 34 distinct ones, background `#34373d`).
- `MultiRegionStyle.build` turns a template into the style of the installed regions (one source per region); `MapFiles.style(theme)` picks
  the template; the theme follows the system dark mode (`MapTheme.DARK/LIGHT`).
- The cursor is `UserArrowIcon`: a blue chevron with a white outline drawn with a `Path` (44 dp), rotated by the course; the idle location
  is a circle layer (`USER_SOURCE`).
- Protomaps also ships other flavours (`white`, `grayscale`, `black`) and a palette of about fifty colours that can be overridden.

## Phase 1: a map that is not too dark (the owner's complaint)

- A pure, JVM-tested **recolourer** (`StyleRecolor`): takes a template and a transform of its colours (brightness, contrast, saturation, hue)
  and returns the template; no regeneration with node, no per-frame cost.
- **Dark presets**: Classic (today), Slate (lighter and softer, the proposed default), Midnight blue, High contrast (brighter roads), Grey.
  A brightness control for the dark map. The light map gets the same mechanism with a couple of variants.
- **Theme choice**: automatic (follows the system, as now), always light, always dark.
- Settings, Appearance, with a live preview; a change reloads the style once (no per-frame work).
- The colours the app puts on top (route casing, 3D buildings, radar and charger layers, night mode of the navigation screen) are adapted
  to the chosen palette.
- Tests: every preset yields valid colours and the same number of layers; the recolourer is deterministic; settings round-trip and are in the
  backup whitelist (plain preferences).

## Phase 2: the cursor

- Settings, Appearance, with a preview. **Shape**: chevron (today), classic arrow, car seen from above, location dot with a direction cone.
  **Colour**: about eight presets. **Size**: small, medium, large. The idle location dot gets the same colour.
- Everything is drawn with `Path` in code (as `UserArrowIcon` is): no image assets, no new licences. Cached once per style load.
- **The icons of Google Maps are NOT copied** (owner asked on 2026-10-10): they are Google's copyrighted and trademarked artwork, only usable
  inside their products; the app is GPLv3 and uses no Google provider (this holds for the `play` flavor too: Play Services gives no right to
  reuse their graphics). The shapes above are the generic ideas (dot with cone, arrow, car), drawn with our own strokes and proportions.
- *Open:* an option to load **the user's own PNG** as the cursor (a local file, never packaged or published; what image is used is the
  user's responsibility). Decision wanted from the owner.

## Phase 3: advanced (only if wanted)

Per-element colours (roads, water, parks), label size, sharing and restoring an appearance, importing a custom style (risk: glyph and sprite
compatibility).

## Open decisions

1. Make *Slate* the default dark map, keeping today's as *Classic*? (proposed: yes)
2. Which cursor shapes besides the chevron? Is the car wanted? Is the user-PNG option wanted?
3. Stop after phase 1 to be tried on a phone, or continue straight to phase 2? (proposed: ship phase 1 in a release candidate and wait)

## Not verifiable without a phone

How a palette looks on the real screen in daylight and at night, and whether the 3D buildings and overlays stay legible: phase 1 therefore
ships with the live preview and is judged by the owner on the device.
